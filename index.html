Hey, I'm Kaif.

I am a computer science graduate, currently working on AI for healthcare as part of a joint project between University College London, AIIMS, and IIT Delhi. I collaborate with clinicians and researchers to bring these fields together in a clinical setting, building language model training infrastructure to pre-train small language models for healthcare in India.
During my undergraduate, I interned with Prof. Prabhat Barnwal at Michigan State University, building a peer knowledge exchange platform for government officers in Punjab. I also worked with Prof. Aaditya Dar on two separate projects, first cleaning and standardizing government asset declaration records, then building a vision-language pipeline to extract data from scanned government affidavits.
Outside of research, I play chess and do photography.
